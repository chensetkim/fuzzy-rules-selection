# Don't combine
Concatenation isn't broken — Sugeno weighted-average aggregation absorbs overlapping rules fine, which is why a3+a6+a9 scores well. It just isn't worth it: +0.003 mean F1 for +8 rules, and it's actually worse than a6 alone on a3 false positives (FPR 0.0063 vs 0.0043). With 18 simulations and no seed variance, a 0.003 difference is well inside the noise you can't currently measure; the 8 extra rules of Z1 flash are real.

# Why not the others
a3 is the overfit one — trained where attacks are rarest (16.8% positive), it collapses to 0.8470 F1 / 0.7436 recall on a6, missing a quarter of attackers. a9 looks great at 2 rules but its FPR blows up to 0.0567 on a6, 13× worse than a6's base — it leans on IF v3 is H THEN VH (support 0.438), which is only clean when attackers are dense.

One correction to what I flagged last turn: the zero expert overlap isn't a representation mismatch. The expert rules evaluate fine here (0.9606 mean F1), so they're in the same term space and the GA could have selected them — it just found a more compact base instead. That's a legitimate result worth reporting, not a bug.
