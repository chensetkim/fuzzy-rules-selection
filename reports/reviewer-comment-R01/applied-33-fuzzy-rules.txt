2H-FuzzRID -- Active Flat Fuzzy Rule Base (fuzzrid_fuzzy_r33_infer_ac_flat, WITH_THFIS=0)
Source: apps/2h-fuzzrid/fuzzrid-fuzzy.c, 33 rules, zero-order Sugeno weighted average, eps=1
Consequent scale: VL=0, L=25, M=50, H=75, VH=100

Inputs (integer scale 0..100):
v1: Rank monotonicity violation
v2: Rank residual / plausibility anomaly
v3: Sustained CUSUM drift
v4: Rank jump
v5: Rank oscillation
v6: Mobility index (context for mobility-explainable anomalies)
v7: Two-hop parent-rank mismatch

Membership functions (membership degrees are integers in 0..100):
L: trapezoidal(v; 0, 0, 20, 50)
M: triangular(v; 20, 50, 80)
H: trapezoidal(v; 50, 80, 100, 100)
not-H: 100 - mu_H(v); full membership through v=50, falling to zero at v=80.
AND uses min; OR uses max. Unmentioned inputs do not constrain a rule.
In rules 31 and 32, (M OR H) means max(mu_M, mu_H), not 100 - mu_L.
Each THEN value is a rule singleton zc[r], not the final aggregated AC.

Notes:
- Dispatch: fuzzrid_fuzzy_infer_ac() calls fuzzrid_fuzzy_r33_infer_ac_flat()
  when WITH_THFIS=0. When WITH_THFIS is nonzero, it calls
  fuzzrid_fuzzy_infer_ac_hfs_x(); the rules above describe only the flat path.
  Calls to fuzzrid_fuzzy_infer_ac_18rule() and fuzzrid_fuzzy_infer_ac_flat()
  are commented out in the dispatcher.
- Aggregation: AC = floor(sum(w[r] * zc[r]) / (1 + sum(w[r]))), r=1..33,
  followed by a clamp to 100. Membership calculations also use integer division.
  If no rule fires, AC=0. The eps=1 denominator means a singleton of 100 does
  not by itself produce an aggregated AC of 100.
- Compared with the former 24-rule report, mobility gates now use not-H
  instead of L. Rule 15 is now v5 is M AND v6 is not-H -> 50; it no longer
  requires v1 is L. Rules 25..32 add coverage, and rule 33 is unconditional
  within this rule base (its firing strength is mu_H(v7)).
- Rules 31 and 32 each remain one rule with an OR antecedent; splitting them
  into separate rules would change the weighted-average result.
- Rule 24 is the only zero-consequent rule: when it fires, it contributes
  weight to the denominator but nothing to the numerator. Other rules with
  lower consequents can also lower the weighted average; rule 24 is not the
  only rule capable of lowering AC.
- Rule 2 assigns a VH singleton to moderate v2 without corroboration.
  Its firing strength peaks at v2=50 and is zero at v2=100; this describes
  the individual rule, not the monotonicity of the complete inference output.
- Historical performance figures documented above fuzzrid_fuzzy_infer_ml()
  in the source are not validation results for this 33-rule base.
